Legal & Trust

PRIVACY POLICY.

Last updated: November 2026

At UNLOST, we believe student data belongs to the student. We are committed to transparency in how information is stored, processed, and safeguarded.

1. Data Storage & Local Persistence

By default, all your personalized roadmap milestones, saved bookmarks, assessment results, and study goals are securely persisted directly in your browser's private client-side local storage. We never sell your personal information or academic aspirations to predatory commercial coaching institutes.

2. Authentication Security

When you create an account, password hashes are generated using cryptographic one-way hashing (SHA-256 with salted digest). Plaintext passwords are never stored in localStorage, Firestore, or logs.

3. Data Export & Deletion

You retain the right to export all your student information at any moment from your Settings page via standard JSON format, or clear your entire workspace history at will.
