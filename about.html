Our Mission

NOT LOST. JUST FINDING MY WAY.

UNLOST is built to dismantle the overwhelming confusion students face when deciding what to study, what entrance exams to attempt, and what career to pursue.

The Problem with Conventional Career Advice

Most high school and college students make critical lifelong decisions under extreme social pressure. Friends follow friends, families enforce narrow expectations, and commercial coaching centers promote hyped rankings and unrealistic promises.

The result? Millions of capable young people find themselves anxious, uncertain, and feeling "behind" before they even begin.

The UNLOST Philosophy

We believe your future isn't a pre-determined destination that someone else hands to you. It's a structure you build from small, daily, deliberate actions.

You don't need all forty years of your professional life mapped out today. You only need enough clarity to take your very next step with confidence.
